# Complex Checklist

Protocol revision: 2.0. Shared rules:
[pattern use](../../03-pattern-use.md) and
[source fidelity](../../04-source-fidelity.md), when its trigger applies.

1. **C1. Define the question and result.** Specify constraints, success criteria,
   consequences of error and material unknowns. Use E.22 when the evaluation
   question itself needs clarification.
2. **C2. Prepare evidence and choose the profile.** Run the skill's gate,
   establish source versions, trust, reading mode and limits; name the specific
   reason for complex depth.
3. **C3. Select checks and dependencies.** Clarify relevant objects, performers
   and relationships. Select viewpoints, routes and sources by the questions
   they must answer; order only actual result dependencies.
4. **C4. Analyze or perform the authorized work.** Obtain evidence, compare
   genuine alternatives/hypotheses where open, and perform authorized actions.
   Keep recommendations provisional while their necessary checks are missing.
5. **C5. Check and revise the affected result.** Test changes appropriately;
   check conclusions against evidence and material counterexamples; reconcile
   viewpoints. Check source implications in both directions when mapping meanings.
6. **C6. Present and stop.** Give the answer/result first, then material evidence,
   alternatives, consequences, limits and useful continuation. Preserve a
   replayable record when its intended use requires one.

Each step records done, not_applicable with reason, or blocked with reason;
the check outcome is separate. Completion of the explanation is not completion
of a described integration test, repair or publication.

Detailed support, loaded only when helpful:
[intake](10-intake-scope.md), [actors](20-actors-roles-work.md),

These are explanations of the six steps, not five additional mandatory passes.
Revisit only affected results when evidence changes.
