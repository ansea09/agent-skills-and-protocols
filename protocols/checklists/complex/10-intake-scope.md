# Intake And Scope Support

Supports C1-C2 of [the complex checklist](00-master.md).

Identify the actual question, expected artifact, material constraints,
consequences and whether clarification is necessary. Separate task admission
from receipt of a message or application startup.

Use A.2.6 for claim scope. Use A.1.1 only for its model-use question, not as a
universal answer-context object. E.22 frames an unclear evaluation question;
framing is not the evaluation itself. A short request can require deep checking.
