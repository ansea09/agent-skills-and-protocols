# Simple-Medium Checklist

Protocol revision: 2.0. Shared rules:
[pattern use](../03-pattern-use.md) and
[source fidelity](../04-source-fidelity.md), the latter when its trigger applies.

1. **S1. Define the question and result.** Identify the expected answer/action,
   material limits and completion criterion. Ask only for missing information
   that can change a material outcome.
2. **S2. Prepare evidence and choose the profile.** Run the installed skill's
   refresh gate, verify protocol trust and the actual reading mode, then select
   simple-medium under classification. Do not confuse validation with refresh.
3. **S3. Select checks.** Select applicable routes/patterns by their questions
   and needed results. Read their relevant bodies and reuse still-valid results.
4. **S4. Answer or perform the authorized work.** Obtain the necessary evidence,
   distinguish facts from assumptions, and produce the requested result.
5. **S5. Check the result.** Check material claims, object/action distinctions,
   scope, time and source fidelity. Escalate material unresolved conflicts.
   Keep an important diagnostic limitation in the answer, not only its trace.
6. **S6. Present and stop.** Give the direct result, material limitation,
   necessary next action and compact basis. Stop under the shared completion rule.

Record each applicable step as done, not_applicable with reason, or blocked
with reason. Record the check outcome separately. Do not print the full record
unless needed for audit. An explanation can be complete while a discussed
operation was not requested or performed.

Example: explaining cached requires its meaning and freshness limit, not a
viewpoint library, a whole-cluster scan or a network refresh solely for the answer.
