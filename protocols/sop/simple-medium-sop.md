# Simple-Medium SOP

The executable steps are in [the checklist](../checklists/simple-medium.md).
Use [shared rules](../03-pattern-use.md) rather than maintaining a second copy.

The profile keeps a bounded task small while preserving evidence and permission
requirements. Read only the patterns needed to resolve the question. Escalation
preserves valid results instead of restarting.

The ordinary output is a direct answer, a useful explanation/example and any
material limitation, followed by a compact basis. These are meanings to convey,
not mandatory headings or empty fields. Do not print an entire ontology or trace
for a simple definition.
