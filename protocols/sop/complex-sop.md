# Complex SOP

The executable steps are in [the checklist](../checklists/complex/00-master.md).
Use [shared rules](../03-pattern-use.md) and conditional
[source fidelity](../04-source-fidelity.md).

Complex means deeper support for material conclusions, not automatically a
longer answer, more viewpoints, every cluster, or several invented alternatives.
Information dependencies can require revisiting a step; unrelated valid work
remains reusable.

Show results before method metadata. Keep the delivered result separate from a
future recommendation or action. E.22 frames an unclear review question; E.23
applies when an actual changed version is evaluated through repeated improvement,
not to every answer or repeat of unchanged input.
